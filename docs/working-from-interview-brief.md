# "Working from [city]" — interviewee brief

The monthly remote-worker interview on `/[city]/remote-work`. One member a
month answers the same seven questions; the piece ends with a session they
will be at. Published from Nate's account as a community post, category
**Working from**, city set. The hub card shows the newest one and hides when
the city has none. See `lib/remoteWork.ts` (INTERVIEW_CATEGORY) for the code
side.

## How to run one

1. Pick the person. Someone who has been here a while and turns up to
   sessions. First choice sets the tone.
2. Confirm the session they will be at before writing anything — the piece
   ends with it, and it must fall 2–4 weeks after publish day.
3. Send the brief below with the three placeholders filled in.
4. Type up the answers, trim to 3–6 sentences each, keep their voice.
   400–600 words total.
5. Send it back for approval. Nothing goes up before they say yes.
6. Admin → Stories & Handbook → New. Kind Community, category Working from,
   city set. Title: `Working from Istanbul: [first name], [role] from
   [where], [neighbourhood]`. Excerpt: the answer to question 4 (the hub card
   quotes it). Cover: their desk photo. Last lines: `Meet [name] at
   [session], [date].` linked to the event, then their one link if they
   gave one.
7. Publish, check the hub card logged in and out, share in the club chat.
8. Ask the interviewee who it should be next; watch the contact inbox for
   nominations labelled "Working from — nomination".

Rules: one optional link, in the sign-off, only if they name it; no pitch
for a service; no exact address of a daily spot; the link never goes in
the excerpt or title.

## The brief (paste, fill the placeholders)

```
Hi [name],

We're starting a monthly interview on the Smileys remote-work page called "Working from Istanbul": one member, seven questions, about what working from here is actually like. I'd love the first one to be you.

How it works
- A few quick facts, then seven questions. Answer in your own words, 3 to 6 sentences each. Voice notes are fine, I'll type them up.
- I'll trim for length and keep your wording, then send it back to you to check.
- One photo of the place you actually work from (question 2), not a headshot. Phone photo is perfect. Check nobody else is recognisable in it.
- It goes on the public site under your first name and the photo, and the photo may appear when the piece is shared. If you'd rather no photo, just say.
- If you'd like, one link at the end (your work, LinkedIn, Instagram, whichever you prefer). Only if you tell me which one.
- It ends with a coworking session or event you'll be at, so people who read it can come and say hi. Pick one in the two to four weeks after [publish date].
- Nothing is published until you've read the final version.

Quick facts (one line each)
- First name, as you'd like it to appear:
- Where you're from:
- What you do, in a few words:
- How long you've been in Istanbul:
- Neighbourhood:
- Optional: who you work for or with (company, clients, your own thing):
- Optional: languages you get by in here:

The questions
1. How did you end up working from Istanbul?
2. Where do you actually work on a Tuesday, and why there? Neighbourhood is enough, no need for the exact address.
3. Which neighbourhood did you pick, and what's the trade-off? The honest version: what you like, what you'd warn someone about.
4. What did nobody tell you before you came? Practical or otherwise: SIM cards, banking, the permit, the ferries, anything.
5. Is it what you expected? What's changed in how you live here since the first month?
6. How did you find people here, and does having a community change how long you stay?
7. One thing someone who landed last week should do this weekend.

Send the answers back by [date] and I'll have it up by [publish date]. Thank you, this is exactly the kind of thing new arrivals keep asking about.

[your name]
```

## Finding the first person

Direct message (better for the first issue):

```
Hi [name], quick one. We're starting a monthly interview on the Smileys remote-work page, "Working from Istanbul": one member, seven short questions about what working from here is really like, and it ends with a session they'll be at so readers can come and say hi.

I'd like you to be the first. You've been here long enough to have opinions, and your answers would actually help someone who landed last week.

Seven questions, 3 to 6 sentences each, voice notes fine, one photo of where you work. Half an hour of your time at most, and nothing goes up before you've read it. Up for it?
```

Club chat post:

```
We're starting something new on the remote-work page: "Working from Istanbul", one member a month, seven questions, honest answers. Where you actually work, which neighbourhood and whether you'd pick it again, what nobody told you before you came.

Looking for the first person. You should have been working from here for a while, have a desk or café you'd defend, and be at a coworking session next month so readers can come and meet you.

It takes about half an hour, voice notes are fine, and you approve the final version. Reply here or message me if that's you, or if you know who it should be.
```

## Other cities

Same brief with the city name swapped. Start a city only once it has a
