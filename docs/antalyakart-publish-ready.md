## Body

**One card, the whole municipal network.** The Antalyakart is Antalya's
contactless transport card. A single card works on the municipal buses, the
AntRay tram that runs the length of the city, the nostalgic tram along the
seafront, and the rest of the municipality's vehicles. Tap the validator as
you board.

**Where to get one.** Cards are sold at the card centres, at the top-up
machines that also sell cards, and at many kiosks and corner shops around the
city. The card centres keep office hours — weekdays 08:30–12:30 and
13:00–18:00, Saturdays 08:30–12:00 — but the machines don't sleep.

**Topping up.** Machines take cash or a credit card. You can also load online
— through the Antalyakart app or the website — with one catch worth knowing:
**an online top-up reaches your card at your first boarding tap**, not
instantly. If you load online on the way to the stop, the balance arrives when
you tap the validator, so it works — but the tap is what completes it.

**Transfers are the part people miss.** Within the transfer window — up to an
hour after your first boarding, with a five-minute protection gap so the
system knows it's a genuine transfer — your next boarding is free or close to
it under the current tariff. Between tram lines the window stretches longer.
The exception matters: **transfers don't apply to disposable cards or to
boardings paid by bank card.** If you're staying, get the actual Antalyakart —
it's the transfers that make it pay.

**No card yet? Your bank card boards the bus.** Contactless credit and debit
cards work on the validators for a full-fare ride. It's the day-one answer
before you've found a card centre — just know you're paying full fare every
time, with no transfer rights.

**The phone-as-card trick — Android only.** The Antalyakart app can turn an
NFC Android phone into your card for a small one-time fee. iPhones can't do
this — Apple restricts NFC — so iOS users stick with the physical card and
use the app for top-ups and balance checks.

**What a ride costs.** Fares change too often for a number written here to
stay true — check the current tariff on the official Antalyakart pages linked
below before you load the card.

**Card types.** Alongside the standard card there's a personalised card
(bring ID and a passport photo to a card centre — it protects your balance if
the card is lost) and discounted cards for students, seniors and teachers.
